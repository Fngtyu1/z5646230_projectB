# AI workflow and student verification notes

## Scope of assistance

Codex was asked to read the Project B brief and Week 9–10 materials, reuse the
student's Project A data foundation, draft the Station 3–4 code, run the official
data, create exhibits, build the Streamlit app, and draft the report. The student
retains responsibility for financial interpretation, authorship, deployment,
and submission. The final numbers are generated by `scripts/run_part_b.py`; none
were typed into the result CSV files by hand.

## How I constrained the assistant

The working contract in `AGENTS.md` requires: past-only expanding estimation,
native-calendar returns before alignment, separate annualisation conventions,
long-only/full-investment checks, a full trading-day sentiment lag, neutral
treatment after a bounded carry, and separation of discovery from holdout. The
deployed app is also prohibited from downloading data or importing NLP packages.

## Critical evaluation and corrections

1. **Innovation selection risk.** A +0.25 sentiment tilt looked slightly better
   in the 2023 holdout at low cost. Choosing it because of that result would leak
   holdout information. I required a 2021–2022 discovery grid; it selected 0.00.
   The +0.25 rule is therefore retained only as an a-priori candidate and is not
   claimed as a deployable edge.
2. **Turnover matters.** The candidate increased 2023 turnover from roughly
   0.27x to 1.04x. Its small benefit disappeared around 25 bps and reversed at
   50 bps. I required one-way cost scenarios and net rather than gross headline
   metrics.
3. **Sentiment model coverage.** Plain VADER scored nearly half the headlines
   exactly neutral. finVADER reduced the exactly-neutral share substantially,
   but this does not prove forecast power. The app separates descriptive tone
   analytics from the governed fusion test.
4. **Chart QA.** The first weights and risk/return charts had crowded legends
   and labels. I asked for the plots to be regenerated with a shared legend and
   fewer annotations, then visually checked the revised PNGs.
5. **App scope.** Any runtime VADER or optimisation would make deployment slow
   and fragile. I required the Streamlit app to use only committed precomputed
   CSVs. An automated Streamlit `AppTest` completed with zero exceptions.
